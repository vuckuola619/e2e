This is a planning inventory. Each row is a separate control prompt with traceability placeholders. The starter applicability value is `UNKNOWN`; replace it with `APPLICABLE`, `PARTIAL`, or `N/A` only after an owner records the condition, rationale, and reassessment trigger. Replace `NOT_RUN` only after the applicable procedure runs and its evidence is reviewed. `Condition to assess` explains when a row is likely relevant; it does not mean the result is already a pass.

## Use notes

The table intentionally covers all 16 canonical areas with 80 independently traceable rows. Select rows by applicability; split or extend a row when the requirement, owner, environment, procedure, or evidence differs. Owner-role labels are placeholders, not assigned people or sign-offs. Split bundled starter rows into specific assertions and procedures before marking any check `PASS`. A check may be `N/A` only after the applicability decision and reassessment trigger are recorded. A task marked complete does not change a `NOT_RUN` check to `PASS`.
